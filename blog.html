List of philosophies of life I have at the age 20

  • everyone is interactable and i’ve tried to use this more. It helps me feel less alone when I know that I could talk to anyone. suddenly the pool of real people around me is so much bigger than it used to be. It used to feel more like a cloud closing in. i’m so happy to know people in so many different communities here. I feel like i have a friend i could talk to about each and every thought.
  • I’ve come to appreciate putting everything about myself out on the platter once it finally feels comfortable to. I hid a lot of myself from everyone I knew for an incredibly long time. I learned that living with so many secrets just made me feel so much more isolated. now, I’ve sent pieces of myself into circulation in so many different spaces. I used to think that’d make me feel scared or exposed but it gives me comfort and a kind of oneness to know that these parts of me exist outside of myself, that those I’d met would be able to piece me together even if i wasnt there. and I realized a lot of the weight I’d been holding in my mind and consequentially in my body was suddenly so much lighter once it was on its own legs in the world
    • counterpoint to myself. I feel like this isn’t true. I think I always wanted to share and be known and know everyone on the deepest level possible. I think I just wasn’t in the kind of spaces where this was possible, or only with a couple people. I really yearn to know everyone deeply like every possible person on the earth. so much undiscovered beauty everyone’s mind is a different work of art. I also have found myself sometimes hating my own art and seeing someone’s “scribbles” so to say and being so moved because it just gave me such an intimate glimpse into their mind… and i just always find any other person’s art so beautiful because it’s not my own. It really fascinates me brings me to a state of awe
    • so much awe
    • The rain makes me euphoric
    • The sun makes me euphoric
  • i’m also becoming aware that i need to befriend myself again. i went out to a restaurant by myself the other day, waiter said how many i said haha, just me :-) it was really calming honestly i like hanging out with myself, i really get me… but i can only do it for a little bit before i start begging everyone to hang out with me socially. something to work on i suppose, i feel like there’s a lot of isolation to come after college that I’m not ready for but i’m sure will be good for me in self discovery and shi. i’m also not ready to live in a single because who will i talk to as i fall asleep. I’m losing the plot of this blog post a little bit i’m very tired. i'm going to cringe at this a little in the morning but it will have been worth it.
